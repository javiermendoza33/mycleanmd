01 · The mark

Logo system

Two lockups and one mark, all drawn as outlines so they set identically everywhere. The wordmark is Bebas Neue with 3% tracking; SKIN is Montserrat Medium with 42% tracking at 30% of the wordmark's cap height. Never retype them.

PrimaryWeb, stationery, wide labels. Default choice.
StackedBoxes, tubes, narrow panels, social.
MarkFavicon, avatar, stickers, back of card.

02 · Using the mark

Clear space, minimum size, don'ts

X = cap height of METHOD
Clear space — one cap height (X) on every side, nothing inside it.
Minimum size — primary 1.1 in / 110 px wide; stacked 0.6 in / 60 px; mark 0.25 in / 24 px.
Colour — ink on white, paper, taupe or sage; white on ink or sage. Never on photographs without a solid panel.

Don't stretch · recolour · fade into a tone with low contrast · retype it (the "LABO" collision in the prototype came from retyping).

05 · Packaging

The numbered grid

Every product front reads the same way, top to bottom: stacked wordmark, then a hairline grid with the product index and category, the name, the actives, the volume and the batch. The grid is the brand as much as the wordmark is — keep its proportions even when a die line changes.

  • 01 Cleansing · 02 Serum · 03 Cream · 04 Balm · 05 Sun · 06 Eyes
  • Rules 0.5 pt ink; cells never share a tone with the background
  • Volume metric first, then fl. oz.; batch "L" + year + index
  • White boxes for cleansers and creams, taupe for serums, sage tubes
  • Caps and droppers in ink-coloured matte
